Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Olympia?

Most small business websites I build cost between $1,500 and $5,000. A simple site with a handful of pages (home, about, services, contact) is on the lower end. Larger sites with custom features, online booking, or a store cost more, and complex custom builds can go above $5,000. I give you a fixed quote after a free consultation, so you know the full cost before we start, and ongoing maintenance is optional starting at $39/month.

Can we meet in person?

Absolutely! If you're in the Olympia, Lacey, or Tumwater area, I'm happy to meet at your office or a local coffee shop, especially for the first planning chat. For clients in Seattle, elsewhere in Washington, or anywhere else in the US, video calls work great, and I'm in the Pacific time zone.

How long does a new website take?

A typical small business website takes 3 to 6 weeks from our first meeting to launch. Larger or more custom projects usually take 6 to 12 weeks. The biggest factor is usually content, like text and photos, so I'll give you a clear timeline and checklist at the start to keep things moving.
